# Changelog

All notable changes to this project are documented in this file.

The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/).

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- `Money` value type in `VademCore`: stores amounts as `Decimal`, rounds to two fraction digits (half away from zero), supports exact addition and subtraction, and encodes to JSON as a string to avoid `Double` precision loss (#4, PR #5)
- `YearMonth` value type in `VademCore`: month arithmetic with year rollover, signed month distance, days in month including leap years, and JSON as `"YYYY-MM"`; supported years are 1...9999 (#7, PR #8)
- `InstallmentPlan` in `VademCore`: splits a total (`Money`) into 1–36 monthly payments starting at a `YearMonth`, with `payments`, `lastMonth` and `amount(in:)`. Each installment is the total divided by the count, rounded down to kuruş, and the leftover kuruş go to the first installment (`1000.00 / 3 → 333.34, 333.33, 333.33`), so payments always sum to the total exactly (ADR-0001). Plans with a non-positive total, a count outside 1–36, or a last installment after 9999-12 are rejected, including when decoding JSON (#15, PR #16)
- `Subscription` in `VademCore`: a recurring charge with an amount, a monthly or yearly period, a renewal day (1–31), a first month and an optional last month, with `charges(in:)`, `amount(in:)` and `chargeDay(in:)`. Yearly subscriptions charge only in the month of their first charge. In shorter months the renewal day moves to the month's last day (day 31 → Feb 28, Feb 29 in leap years, Apr 30). Subscriptions with a non-positive amount, a renewal day outside 1–31, or a last month before the first are rejected, including when decoding JSON (#19, PR #20)
- `Card` in `VademCore`: a credit card with a display name (trimmed, 1–40 characters), a statement day and a due day (1–31), and a stable `id`; no card numbers are stored. `statementDay(in:)` moves the statement day to the month's last day in shorter months, and `dueDate(forStatementIn:)` returns the first due day strictly after the statement date, in the same month or the next (statement 25, due 5 in March → April 5; the same day counts as the next month). Cards with an empty or too-long name or a day outside 1–31 are rejected, including when decoding JSON (#23, PR #24)
- Tracked items and a storage contract in `VademCore`: `InstallmentItem` (always on a card) and `SubscriptionItem` (card optional) give a plan or subscription an `id` and a title (trimmed, 1–60 characters, also checked when decoding JSON). `PaymentRepository` defines how cards, installments and subscriptions are saved and loaded: save replaces by `id`, a card still used by an item cannot be deleted (`cardInUse`), an item cannot point to an unsaved card (`unknownCard`), and lists have a fixed order (text compared with `caseInsensitiveCompare`, not localized, then `id`). `InMemoryPaymentRepository` is the reference implementation for previews and tests; storage on disk comes next (#26, PR #27)
- Cards, installments and subscriptions are now stored on device with SwiftData: `SwiftDataPaymentRepository` in `VademPersistence` follows the same `PaymentRepository` contract as the in-memory reference (all 29 shared scenarios pass). The store lives in the App Group container with no iCloud sync, data survives reopening, amounts are stored as exact decimal text (never `Double`), every write is saved immediately, and corrupt stored records fail with an error instead of crashing. The schema is versioned from the start (`VademSchemaV1`, `1.0.0`) with a migration plan. Not yet used by any screen (#30, PR #31)
- Design system "Ink and Vade" in `VademFeatures`: 13 color tokens with light and dark values (`Color` statics), six mesh-gradient card styles (`CardStyle`, picked per card from its id, the same on every launch), `CardFaceView`, `AmountText` (Turkish lira format `₺1.234,56`, read by VoiceOver as "1.234,56 Türk lirası"), `DueChip` (today / soon / later) and a glass modifier for floating controls (iOS 26 glass, material on older versions). The card drift stops under Reduce Motion. A design gallery (Debug builds, `-ui-gallery`) shows every piece. Still open (#36): the accessibility audit reports low contrast for card text on four of the six styles, and in light mode the navigation bar turns dark when a dark card scrolls under it. Not yet used by any app screen (#33, PR #38)
- `MonthlySchedule` in `VademCore`: answers "what is due in this month?" from cards, installments and subscriptions, grouped by due date (ADR-0002). An installment is due in its statement month's due date (`Card.dueDate(forStatementIn:)`), so only the previous and the current statement month can fall in a month. A subscription on a card is charged to the statement on or after its charge day (after the statement day it goes to the next statement); one without a card is due on its charge date. `entries` are ordered by due day, title (`caseInsensitiveCompare`), id and installment number; `total` and `total(for:)` are exact `Decimal` sums. Items whose card is unknown are skipped, and a due date beyond year 9999 is skipped instead of crashing. Not yet used by any screen (#42, PR #43)
- Home shows an upcoming months strip: a horizontal row of 6 months starting at the selected month, each chip with the month's total (empty months show zero). Tapping a chip selects that month for the list below, and previous/next stay in sync. New `UpcomingMonths` in `VademCore` builds the schedules and clamps at the last supported month. VoiceOver reads each chip with the month, the total ("Türk lirası") and a selected trait; the scroll honors Reduce Motion and `-ui-static-motion`, and the strip scrolls sideways at the largest text size (#50, PR #51)
- Home shows a month calendar below the upcoming strip: a grid with the month's days (weekday columns follow the locale's first weekday), each due day marked with its exact total. Tapping a day filters the payment list to that day and tapping it again ("Show all payments") clears the filter; a day without payments shows an empty-day message, and changing the month clears the selection. VoiceOver reads each day with its total. New in `VademCore`: `MonthlySchedule.days`, `entries(onDay:)`, `total(onDay:)` and `YearMonth.weekdayOfFirstDay`. The grid adapts at the largest text size and has no animations. Open: the UI check still reports 7 contrast issues on the Home calendar parts (#53, PR #55)
- Home has a "+" button (`Add installment`) that opens a form for a new installment plan: title, card, total amount, number of installments (1–36) and first month. Each invalid field shows its error and Save stays disabled until the form is valid; saving stores the plan through `PaymentRepository` and Home reloads when the sheet closes. Validation lives in `VademCore` (`InstallmentDraft`); the amount follows the locale decimal separator (at most 12 integer and 6 fraction digits). Card and month choices open list screens instead of system menus, which failed the accessibility audit at large text sizes. Known limitation: there is no way to add a card yet, so on a fresh store Save stays disabled ("Add a card first."). Debug launch arguments `-ui-add-form` and `-ui-add-form-errors` open the form for capture (#62, PR #63)
- Cards can now be added in the app: an "Add a card" sheet asks for a name (1–40 characters), a statement day and a due day (1–31), shows an error under each invalid field and keeps Save disabled until the form is valid. It opens from an "Add a card" row in the installment form's card list (the new card is selected after saving) and from an "Add a card" prompt on Home when no card exists (Home reloads when the sheet closes). Validation lives in `VademCore` (`CardDraft`). The debug launch argument `-ui-add-card-form` opens the form for capture. Known limitations: the name field strips `\n` but not `\r`, it has no Done/submit action, and the Home prompt has no automated capture (#65, PR #68)
- Payments and cards can be edited and deleted. On Home, tapping a payment row or a card subtotal opens its form, prefilled, with the same validation; saving keeps the item's `id`. The edit screen has a Delete row behind a confirmation alert (an alert, because `confirmationDialog` rendered as a small clipped popover on iOS 26). Deleting a card that still has payments is refused with a message (`RepositoryError.cardInUse`). Home reloads when the sheet closes. Known limitation: a card without payments in the month does not appear in Home's subtotals, so an unused card cannot be deleted from the UI yet; a card list screen would need its own issue. A subscription's last month is dropped silently when its first month is moved past it (#67, PR #74)
- Design refresh step 1: color tokens moved to a teal palette (mint in light, deep teal in dark). 13 existing color sets updated and five added: `hero`, `heroText`, `heroSecondary`, `accent` (fill only) and `onAccent`, with matching `Color.vademHero`, `vademHeroText`, `vademHeroSecondary`, `vademAccent`, `vademOnAccent`. Tokens only; components are not restyled yet. The new text/background pairs are covered by `TokenContrastTests` (all at least 4.5:1, tightest `heroSecondary` on `hero` at 4.86:1 in light) (#77, PR #79)
- Design refresh step 2: four building blocks in `VademFeatures/Design/`, shown in the debug design gallery (Hero, Rows, Pills sections) and not yet used by Home or the forms. `HeroPanel` shows the month total, a progress bar and caption, and an action slot (`progress(paid:total:)` clamps to 0...1, total 0 gives 0); `HeroPill` is a capsule button style; `PaymentRow` shows a day circle, title, subtitle, amount and `DueChip`, and stacks at accessibility text sizes; `SurfaceCard` groups rows on the surface color with hairline separators. Only color tokens are used, and accent is never text. VoiceOver reads the hero panel as one element and each row as one element (#82, PR #83)
- Design refresh step 3: Home uses the new components. A hero panel ("Due this month") shows the month total with a progress bar and the caption "Due so far X of Y"; progress is the share of the month's amount already due, since there is no paid tracking (past month = all, future month = 0, the day filter does not change it). The toolbar "+" is replaced by an "Add" pill in the hero that keeps the add menu (installment or subscription) and also shows in the error state; a "Calendar" pill scrolls to the calendar and is hidden when there is none. Payment rows use `PaymentRow`, and rows, "By card" subtotals and the add-card prompt sit on surface cards. Identifiers and VoiceOver labels are unchanged. Open: no UI test yet for the error-state hero or the hidden Calendar pill (#85, PR #86)
- Design refresh step 4: the installment, subscription and card forms (add and edit) use the surface-card look. Fields sit in one card with hairline separators, and the save/delete error and the Delete button have their own cards. The forms are a `ScrollView` with shared parts in `FormRows.swift` (`FormCard`, `FormScroll`, `FormField`, `FormFailureRow`) instead of a `Form`, because a `Form` with one card section failed the Dynamic Type and clipped-text audit. Tapping anywhere in a text row focuses the field. Delete uses the `dueToday` color instead of `Color.red`, chevrons use `brand`, and option lists (still a `List`) have surface rows with separators. `SurfaceCard` gets a defaulted `rowVerticalPadding`; new contrast pair `dueToday` on `surface`. View models, validation, flows and accessibility identifiers are unchanged. Open: no UI test for tap-to-focus (#88, PR #89)
- Design refresh step 5 (last): the upcoming months chips and the Home calendar use the new look. Unselected chips are surface with a hairline border and the selected chip uses the hero fill; the calendar grid sits on a surface card, the selected day is a hero circle, today has a brand ring and payment dots use the brand color. Identifiers, VoiceOver labels, 44 pt hit areas and static motion are unchanged. Calendar columns are about 45.6 pt wide on 375 pt iPhones (a first version gave about 42 pt). New capture test `testHomeSelectedDay` shows the selected day and chip. Open: it does not assert the selected state (#91, PR #92)
- Reminder planner in `VademCore` (`NotificationPlanner.plan`), first part of notifications: from cards, installments and subscriptions it computes which reminders to schedule, a day-before and a due-day reminder per due date at 09:00 in the calendar's time zone. Past fire dates (including exactly now) are dropped, same-day payments are grouped per due date and kind (count and exact `Decimal` total, or the single payment's title), and only the nearest 63 are kept (iOS allows 64 pending). Ids are deterministic (`due-YYYY-MM-DD-today|dayBefore`), the amount formatter is injected, and the day-before reminder of a payment due on the 1st just past the lookahead is still planned. A negative lookahead gives an empty plan. Pure logic, with DST tests; nothing is scheduled yet and there is no permission prompt or setting (scheduling and permission flow, then settings, come next). Known limitation: `lookaheadMonths: .max` overflows (#95, PR #96)
- Reminders are now scheduled on the device, second part of notifications. After a payment or card is saved, edited or deleted (when the form closes) and whenever the app becomes active, including a cold launch, the app plans reminders with `NotificationPlanner` and replaces its pending notifications (ids starting `due-`). The system permission is asked once per launch, only when there is something to remind, in practice right after the first payment is saved; if it is denied nothing is scheduled. New in `VademFeatures`: `ReminderCenter` protocol, `SystemReminderCenter` (the only file importing `UserNotifications`) and `ReminderScheduler`; overlapping refreshes merge into one extra pass and a failing add does not skip the rest. Sample-data and gallery launches never create a scheduler. Open: the real permission alert and delivered notifications have no automated test (checked by hand); the settings toggle comes next (#98, PR #99)
- Reminder settings, last part of notifications. A gear button next to the app title on Home opens a Settings sheet with a reminders toggle, a timing choice (day before, due day, or both) and, when the system permission is denied, a message with an "Open Settings" button. The choice is stored on device (`ReminderPreferences`, UserDefaults) and applied the next time reminders are refreshed; `NotificationPlanner.plan` gets a `kinds` parameter for it. Debug launch arguments `-ui-settings` and `-ui-settings-denied` open the sheet for capture. Open: the real denied state and system alert are not automated (a fake status drives the denied capture) (#101, PR #102)
- `PaymentSummary` in `VademCore`, first part of the widget and Siri work: a pure summary of the current month from cards, installments and subscriptions. It gives the month total, the amount still to pay (`remaining`, `remainingCount`; a payment due today counts as remaining, earlier days do not) and the next payments (`next`, at most 3 by default, sorted by due day, title and id, looking ahead 3 months so it is not empty at month end, `daysUntil` 0 = today). Spoken helpers build the sentences Siri will read, "You pay X in October 2026, Y still to go." and "Next: Rent, X, today / tomorrow / in N days / on 3 November.", with only the first upcoming payment read; amounts come from an injected formatter. No widget or App Intent exists yet (App Intents, then the widget extension, come next). Known limitation: the English month-name table duplicates the one in `HomeViewModel` (#105, PR #106)
- Siri and Shortcuts can now answer "how much do I pay this month?" and "what is my next payment?", second part of the widget and Siri work. Two App Intents, `MonthTotalIntent` and `NextPaymentIntent`, answer without opening the app and are offered as app shortcuts (`VademShortcuts`, four English phrases each, all containing the app name). The answers come from the new `PaymentSummaryProvider` in `VademFeatures`, which builds a `PaymentSummary` from the repository and returns the spoken sentences; if the store cannot be read both intents say "I could not open your payments." instead of failing. The sentences are English with Turkish lira amounts. Open: the phrases and spoken output have no automated test (check in the Shortcuts app with Vadem open and closed), and each intent call opens its own `ModelContainer` on the App Group store, so duplicate-store warnings are possible. The widget comes next (#108, PR #109)
- Home-screen widget, last part of the widget and Siri work. A new `VademWidget` extension offers "Upcoming payments" in three sizes: small (month total and next payment), medium (month total, amount left and up to three upcoming payments) and lock-screen rectangular. It reads the same App Group store, shows empty and unavailable states, and each row is one VoiceOver element. `WidgetTimelineBuilder` in `VademFeatures` prepares entries for now and the next three midnights so "Today / Tomorrow / in N days" stay right, and retries after an hour if the store cannot be read. The app reloads the widget after data changes and when it becomes active (`AppRefresh`, `WidgetReloading`). Open: signing needs the App Group enabled for the new bundle id `com.alicankurt.Vadem.VademWidget`; rendering in all sizes, light/dark and the store access from the extension are not automated (check by hand); each timeline call opens its own `ModelContainer` (#111, PR #112)
- Analytics screen: a chart button in Home's toolbar opens a bar chart of the next 12 months starting with the current one, each bar stacked into installments and subscriptions, current month highlighted. Selecting a bar shows that month's total and its two parts in a summary card above the chart; with no payments the screen shows a message and a button to add one. Each bar has a VoiceOver label (month and amount) and the chart has an `accessibilityChartDescriptor`; it honors `-ui-static-motion`. New `AnalyticsViewModel` (loading, empty and error states) in `VademFeatures`. Known limitations: legend and VoiceOver strings are not localizable yet (no string catalog), capture covers the top of the screen only, and VoiceOver may read the chart label and value twice (check on a device) (#116, PR #120)
- Analytics shows a donut of the selected month by card (each card in its style color, payments without a card as their own share), legend rows with name, amount and percent (summing to 100), and a "Last installment payment" insight card ("Last payment this month" or "Paid off"; hidden without installments). Selecting a bar updates all of it. VoiceOver reads the donut as one element, and the per-card details are in the legend rows, which stack vertically at accessibility text sizes. Known limitation: the functional test selects the bar with a long press, which may flake (#117, PR #122)

### Fixed

- The version label on the root screen now reads "Version 0.1.0" in a smaller primary-colored style, so it passes the accessibility audit (contrast, readable label) in light, dark and the largest Dynamic Type size; UI checks assert its text, position and size (#10, PR #12)

### Pipeline

- `scripts/ui-check.sh` now renders dark mode (set via `simctl` per appearance), reports every audit failure per test instead of only the first, and the dark-mode guard checks pixel brightness instead of comparing PNG bytes (PR #11)
- UI capture is leaner: the orchestrator runs `scripts/ui-check.sh` in the background during `/ship` and reads only its summary lines; the `ui-checker` agent is off, and the test writer and reviewer run on a cheaper model. `UI_CHECK_ONLY=<screens>` captures only the listed screens, and 600 px copies of every screenshot go to `screenshots/small/`. Scrolling to a marker stays below the navigation bar and no longer oscillates; an unreachable marker reports its frame, the bar bottom and the attempts. Audit issues on elements not fully visible are `[a11y-hidden]` notes and identical consecutive parts are `[ui-note]` (both in `notes.txt`), not failures. A full gallery capture takes about 4 min (was about 10), with a 180 s limit per test against XCTest hangs (#37, PR #41)
- UI check contrast findings are now notes instead of failures, because Apple's contrast audit gives different results on repeated runs of the same tree. `[a11y-contrast]` means the finding reproduced on a second measurement, `[a11y-flaky]` means it did not; both go to `notes.txt`, and every other audit type still fails. Element-less findings list the labels of the content under the status bar. Contrast is now guaranteed by `TokenContrastTests` (11 token pairs, light and dark, at least 4.5:1) next to `CardContrastTests`. Home's 5 element-less findings come from the scrolled calendar passing under the status bar (since #53) (#59, PR #60)
- UI check handles two edge cases found while shipping #67: system alert labels and the alert-based Delete flow no longer fail the audit or stale main-screen tests (PR #73), and a Dynamic Type "partially unsupported" finding on rows low in a lazy list is reported as an `[a11y-lazy]` note instead of a failure, because the same row passes higher up the screen; the cause is a hypothesis, not proven (PR #75)
- The full gallery capture was split into `testGalleryScreen` and a second test so each stays under the 3 minute per-test allowance, which the new palette exceeded (PR #78)
