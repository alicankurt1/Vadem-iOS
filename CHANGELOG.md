## [Unreleased]

### Added

- `Money` value type in `VademCore`: stores amounts as `Decimal`, rounds to two fraction digits (half away from zero), supports exact addition and subtraction, and encodes to JSON as a string to avoid `Double` precision loss (#4, PR #5)
- `YearMonth` value type in `VademCore`: month arithmetic with year rollover, signed month distance, days in month including leap years, and JSON as `"YYYY-MM"`; supported years are 1...9999 (#7, PR #8)
- `InstallmentPlan` in `VademCore`: splits a total (`Money`) into 1–36 monthly payments starting at a `YearMonth`, with `payments`, `lastMonth` and `amount(in:)`. Each installment is the total divided by the count, rounded down to kuruş, and the leftover kuruş go to the first installment (`1000.00 / 3 → 333.34, 333.33, 333.33`), so payments always sum to the total exactly (ADR-0001). Plans with a non-positive total, a count outside 1–36, or a last installment after 9999-12 are rejected, including when decoding JSON (#15, PR #16)
- `Subscription` in `VademCore`: a recurring charge with an amount, a monthly or yearly period, a renewal day (1–31), a first month and an optional last month, with `charges(in:)`, `amount(in:)` and `chargeDay(in:)`. Yearly subscriptions charge only in the month of their first charge. In shorter months the renewal day moves to the month's last day (day 31 → Feb 28, Feb 29 in leap years, Apr 30). Subscriptions with a non-positive amount, a renewal day outside 1–31, or a last month before the first are rejected, including when decoding JSON (#19, PR #20)
- `Card` in `VademCore`: a credit card with a display name (trimmed, 1–40 characters), a statement day and a due day (1–31), and a stable `id`; no card numbers are stored. `statementDay(in:)` moves the statement day to the month's last day in shorter months, and `dueDate(forStatementIn:)` returns the first due day strictly after the statement date, in the same month or the next (statement 25, due 5 in March → April 5; the same day counts as the next month). Cards with an empty or too-long name or a day outside 1–31 are rejected, including when decoding JSON (#23, PR #24)
- Tracked items and a storage contract in `VademCore`: `InstallmentItem` (always on a card) and `SubscriptionItem` (card optional) give a plan or subscription an `id` and a title (trimmed, 1–60 characters, also checked when decoding JSON). `PaymentRepository` defines how cards, installments and subscriptions are saved and loaded: save replaces by `id`, a card still used by an item cannot be deleted (`cardInUse`), an item cannot point to an unsaved card (`unknownCard`), and lists have a fixed order (text compared with `caseInsensitiveCompare`, not localized, then `id`). `InMemoryPaymentRepository` is the reference implementation for previews and tests; storage on disk comes next (#26, PR #27)
- Cards, installments and subscriptions are now stored on device with SwiftData: `SwiftDataPaymentRepository` in `VademPersistence` follows the same `PaymentRepository` contract as the in-memory reference (all 29 shared scenarios pass). The store lives in the App Group container with no iCloud sync, data survives reopening, amounts are stored as exact decimal text (never `Double`), every write is saved immediately, and corrupt stored records fail with an error instead of crashing. The schema is versioned from the start (`VademSchemaV1`, `1.0.0`) with a migration plan. Not yet used by any screen (#30, PR #31)
- Design system "Ink and Vade" in `VademFeatures`: 13 color tokens with light and dark values (`Color` statics), six mesh-gradient card styles (`CardStyle`, picked per card from its id, the same on every launch), `CardFaceView`, `AmountText` (Turkish lira format `₺1.234,56`, read by VoiceOver as "1.234,56 Türk lirası"), `DueChip` (today / soon / later) and a glass modifier for floating controls (iOS 26 glass, material on older versions). The card drift stops under Reduce Motion. A design gallery (Debug builds, `-ui-gallery`) shows every piece. Still open (#36): the accessibility audit reports low contrast for card text on four of the six styles, and in light mode the navigation bar turns dark when a dark card scrolls under it. Not yet used by any app screen (#33, PR #38)
- `MonthlySchedule` in `VademCore`: answers "what is due in this month?" from cards, installments and subscriptions, grouped by due date (ADR-0002). An installment is due in its statement month's due date (`Card.dueDate(forStatementIn:)`), so only the previous and the current statement month can fall in a month. A subscription on a card is charged to the statement on or after its charge day (after the statement day it goes to the next statement); one without a card is due on its charge date. `entries` are ordered by due day, title (`caseInsensitiveCompare`), id and installment number; `total` and `total(for:)` are exact `Decimal` sums. Items whose card is unknown are skipped, and a due date beyond year 9999 is skipped instead of crashing. Not yet used by any screen (#42, PR #43)
- Home shows an upcoming months strip: a horizontal row of 6 months starting at the selected month, each chip with the month's total (empty months show zero). Tapping a chip selects that month for the list below, and previous/next stay in sync. New `UpcomingMonths` in `VademCore` builds the schedules and clamps at the last supported month. VoiceOver reads each chip with the month, the total ("Türk lirası") and a selected trait; the scroll honors Reduce Motion and `-ui-static-motion`, and the strip scrolls sideways at the largest text size (#50, PR #51)
- Home shows a month calendar below the upcoming strip: a grid with the month's days (weekday columns follow the locale's first weekday), each due day marked with its exact total. Tapping a day filters the payment list to that day and tapping it again ("Show all payments") clears the filter; a day without payments shows an empty-day message, and changing the month clears the selection. VoiceOver reads each day with its total. New in `VademCore`: `MonthlySchedule.days`, `entries(onDay:)`, `total(onDay:)` and `YearMonth.weekdayOfFirstDay`. The grid adapts at the largest text size and has no animations. Open: the UI check still reports 7 contrast issues on the Home calendar parts (#53, PR #55)
- Home has a "+" button (`Add installment`) that opens a form for a new installment plan: title, card, total amount, number of installments (1–36) and first month. Each invalid field shows its error and Save stays disabled until the form is valid; saving stores the plan through `PaymentRepository` and Home reloads when the sheet closes. Validation lives in `VademCore` (`InstallmentDraft`); the amount follows the locale decimal separator (at most 12 integer and 6 fraction digits). Card and month choices open list screens instead of system menus, which failed the accessibility audit at large text sizes. Known limitation: there is no way to add a card yet, so on a fresh store Save stays disabled ("Add a card first."). Debug launch arguments `-ui-add-form` and `-ui-add-form-errors` open the form for capture (#62, PR #63)
- Cards can now be added in the app: an "Add a card" sheet asks for a name (1–40 characters), a statement day and a due day (1–31), shows an error under each invalid field and keeps Save disabled until the form is valid. It opens from an "Add a card" row in the installment form's card list (the new card is selected after saving) and from an "Add a card" prompt on Home when no card exists (Home reloads when the sheet closes). Validation lives in `VademCore` (`CardDraft`). The debug launch argument `-ui-add-card-form` opens the form for capture. Known limitations: the name field strips `\n` but not `\r`, it has no Done/submit action, and the Home prompt has no automated capture (#65, PR #68)

### Fixed

- The version label on the root screen now reads "Version 0.1.0" in a smaller primary-colored style, so it passes the accessibility audit (contrast, readable label) in light, dark and the largest Dynamic Type size; UI checks assert its text, position and size (#10, PR #12)

### Pipeline

- `scripts/ui-check.sh` now renders dark mode (set via `simctl` per appearance), reports every audit failure per test instead of only the first, and the dark-mode guard checks pixel brightness instead of comparing PNG bytes (PR #11)
- UI capture is leaner: the orchestrator runs `scripts/ui-check.sh` in the background during `/ship` and reads only its summary lines; the `ui-checker` agent is off, and the test writer and reviewer run on a cheaper model. `UI_CHECK_ONLY=<screens>` captures only the listed screens, and 600 px copies of every screenshot go to `screenshots/small/`. Scrolling to a marker stays below the navigation bar and no longer oscillates; an unreachable marker reports its frame, the bar bottom and the attempts. Audit issues on elements not fully visible are `[a11y-hidden]` notes and identical consecutive parts are `[ui-note]` (both in `notes.txt`), not failures. A full gallery capture takes about 4 min (was about 10), with a 180 s limit per test against XCTest hangs (#37, PR #41)
- UI check contrast findings are now notes instead of failures, because Apple's contrast audit gives different results on repeated runs of the same tree. `[a11y-contrast]` means the finding reproduced on a second measurement, `[a11y-flaky]` means it did not; both go to `notes.txt`, and every other audit type still fails. Element-less findings list the labels of the content under the status bar. Contrast is now guaranteed by `TokenContrastTests` (11 token pairs, light and dark, at least 4.5:1) next to `CardContrastTests`. Home's 5 element-less findings come from the scrolled calendar passing under the status bar (since #53) (#59, PR #60)
